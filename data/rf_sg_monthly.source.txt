rf_sg_monthly.csv — provenance
================================

Source   : Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS), Financial Database,
           "Domestic Interest Rates" (daily), column "SORA".
Page     : https://eservices.mas.gov.sg/statistics/dir/DomesticInterestRates.aspx
Export   : "Domestic Interest Rates.csv" downloaded from that page on 2026-10-07.
Coverage : 2005-07 .. 2026-10 (daily SORA).

Transformation
--------------
1. Forward-fill the Year/Month columns (they appear only on the first row of
   each month in the source export) and parse the daily value date.
2. Average the daily SORA quotes (% per annum) within each calendar month.
3. Convert to a monthly decimal risk-free return:  mean_daily_SORA / 100 / 12.

Columns: Date (YYYY-MM), RF (decimal monthly rate).

Notes
-----
SORA is an overnight rate; dividing the monthly-average annualised rate by 12
follows the course handout ("monthly rate = annual/12 is fine"). The series is a
risk-free proxy for the SGD universe, consistent with the FF3 monthly RF used by
the US universe.
